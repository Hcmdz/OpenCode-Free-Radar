OpenCode Free Radar

Android Kotlin MinSDK 29 TargetSDK 36

Native Android app that tracks AI models free to use with OpenCode and notifies you when new free offers appear.

Why It Exists

Free AI model offers appear and disappear constantly across providers, and checking them by hand is tedious. OpenCode Free Radar watches the public catalogs for you — usable-free statuses, favorites, and alerts — so you never miss a model you can run with OpenCode for free.

Catalog Sync

Daily sync from public indexes: models.dev, OpenRouter, the LiteLLM price map, and the OpenCode Zen roster.

Usable-Free Statuses

Every model flagged FREE, LIMITED, TRIAL, or TEMPORARY; paid and expired offers filtered out.

OpenCode Compatibility

Tool-calling, vision, and structured-output flags per model.

Offline-First

Room database, favorites, filters, and per-model price history — all on device.

Freebie Alerts

Background sync with notifications when new free offers appear.

In-App Updates

Daily check via GitHub Releases with SHA-256 verified download and install.

Material You

Dynamic color, edge-to-edge layout, dark theme, English/French/Arabic with RTL.

New in 0.2.0

Collapsing header with live sync age, favorites filter, recent/name/context sorting, first-sync skeleton, and immersive fullscreen.
